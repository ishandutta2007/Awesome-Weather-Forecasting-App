# Top Weather Forecasting App Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Forecast Accuracy, Radar Visualization & Privacy-First Weather Data*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Weather Forecasting**. These tools provide current conditions, hourly and extended forecasts, radar imagery, severe weather alerts, and atmospheric data for everyday planning and professional meteorology.

**Examples** include Windows Weather, AccuWeather, The Weather Channel, Carrot Weather, Weather Underground, Windy, RadarOmega, Overdrop, Yahoo Weather, and Dark Sky (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Weather is one of the strongest open-source domains, with **Breezy Weather** leading as the most feature-rich Android app supporting 50+ weather sources . **Raindrop** brings sophisticated terminal-based forecasting , while **OpenStorm**, **Supercell Wx**, and **Hookecho** deliver advanced radar visualization for storm chasers and meteorologists . This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[AccuWeather](https://www.accuweather.com/)**  
  Commercial weather forecasting service with proprietary MinuteCast precipitation predictions and global coverage. Ad-supported free tier; premium subscription removes ads. **Closed source** with API access for developers.

- **[The Weather Channel](https://weather.com/)**  
  IBM-owned weather platform with comprehensive forecasts, radar, and severe weather coverage. Uses proprietary forecast models and Watson AI for some products. Free with ads; premium tier available.

- **[Carrot Weather](https://www.meetcarrot.com/weather/)**  
  iOS/macOS weather app known for its humorous personality and customizable interface. Uses data from multiple sources. **Premium subscription** required for full features. Closed source.

- **[Weather Underground](https://www.wunderground.com/)**  
  Community-driven weather platform with personal weather station (PWS) network. Provides hyperlocal data from user-contributed sensors. Free with ads; ad-free subscription available.

- **[Windy](https://www.windy.com/)**  
  Visualization-focused weather platform with animated wind, precipitation, and wave maps. Popular with sailors, pilots, and outdoor enthusiasts. Free web/app with premium tier. **Uses open data sources** like GFS, ICON, and ECMWF .

- **[RadarOmega](https://www.radaromega.com/)**  
  Professional-grade radar app for storm chasers and meteorologists. Features advanced radar products, storm tracking, and custom alerts. **Subscription-based** with one-time purchase options. Closed source.

- **[Overdrop](https://overdrop.app/)**  
  Beautiful Android weather app with widgets and customizable themes. Uses multiple weather sources. Free with premium subscription. Closed source.

- **[Yahoo Weather](https://www.yahoo.com/news/weather/)**  
  Yahoo's weather app with clean design and Flickr photo integration for locations. Free with ads. Closed source.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Breezy Weather](https://github.com/breezy-weather/breezy-weather)**  
  **The leading open-source Android weather app**, with 50+ weather sources, Material 3 Expressive design, and full privacy controls . LGPL-3.0 licensed. Features 16-day forecasts, hourly data, precipitation nowcasting, air quality, pollen, severe weather alerts, 10+ widget options, live wallpaper, and custom icon pack support. **Fully works with Open-Meteo** (FOSS source) and collects no personal data. Available via GitHub, F-Droid, and Obtainium — intentionally not on Google Play to maintain open-source principles . **The de facto open-source alternative to commercial weather apps**.

- **[Raindrop](https://github.com/binarydoubling/raindrop)**  
  Beautiful, feature-rich **weather CLI for the terminal** with no API key required . Python 3.12+. Features sparkline graphs for temperature/precipitation trends, technical analysis (EMA crossovers, rate-of-change), real driving route weather via OpenStreetMap, full-screen live dashboard, astronomical data (moon phases, golden hour), marine forecasts, and clothing recommendations. **The most sophisticated terminal weather tool** — ideal for developers and power users.

- **[OSS Weather](https://github.com/ossappscollective/oss-weather)**  
  Lightweight, privacy-first Android weather app with **no ads, no tracking, no analytics** . MIT licensed. Supports multiple providers (OpenWeather, Open-Meteo, AccuWeather, Meteo France), animated radar maps, home screen widgets, offline caching, and customizable UI. **The best choice for Android users prioritizing minimalism and privacy**.

- **[OpenStorm](https://github.com/JordanSchlick/OpenStorm)**  
  Free and open-source **3D radar viewer** using Unreal Engine 5 with custom volumetric ray marching shaders . Displays full 3D Level 2 radar data with multithreaded loading, real-time file updates, base and derived products (Reflectivity, Velocity, Spectrum Width, Rotation), interpolation, and **VR headset support**. Supports NEXRAD and ODIM H5 data. **The most visually advanced open-source radar viewer**.

- **[Supercell Wx](https://github.com/rcg4u/supercell-wx)**  
  Free, open-source **advanced weather radar viewer** for NEXRAD Level 2 and 3 data with severe weather alerts . Cross-platform (Windows, Linux, macOS). Displays continuously updating data on a responsive map with reflectivity, velocity, and other products. Requires free MapTiler or Mapbox API key for map tiles. **The go-to choice for storm chasers wanting a lightweight, native application**.

- **[Hookecho](https://github.com/d4vid87/hookecho)**  
  Advanced NEXRAD radar viewer in **Rust** with Level 2/3 analysis, MRMS, future radar (HRRR), and warning intelligence . Features GPU polar pipeline, all six Level 2 moments, velocity dealiasing, storm-relative velocity, **GRLevelX .pal color table support**, four-pane split view, cross-sections, archive timeline reaching back to June 1991, and warning verification lab (POD, FAR, CSI). Available on Windows, Linux, and Android. **The most technically sophisticated open-source radar app**.

- **[FluentWeather](https://github.com/derfloh205/FluentWeather)**  
  Beautiful UWP weather app for Windows with modern Fluent Design . Multiple weather sources, widgets, and customizable interface. **The best open-source weather app for Windows users**.

- **[Mousam](https://github.com/amitmerchant1990/mousam)**  
  Beautiful, minimal, and fast weather app using Open-Meteo API . MPL-2.0 licensed. Requires Android 6.0+. **Lightweight and clean** for users wanting simplicity.

- **[wX](https://github.com/WX-PH/wX)**  
  Free, no-ads, open-source advanced weather application for **storm chasers, meteorologists, and weather enthusiasts** . GNU GPLv3 licensed. Provides detailed atmospheric data, radar, and severe weather tools. **Professional-grade open-source option**.

- **[Bura](https://github.com/Webini/bura)**  
  Transforms and visualizes weather data from Open-Meteo.com with essential information at a glance and deep-dive graphs . **Clean, modern open-source weather app** for Android.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Geometric Weather** — Light and powerful weather app with real-time temperature, air quality, and 15-day forecast . Note: discontinued but source available.
- **WeatherMaster** — Weather app for Android inspired by Google Pixel weather design .
- **Ocula** — Weather app built with modern web standards as a PWA template for developers .
- **wttr.in** — Console-oriented weather service supporting ANSI, HTML, and PNG output for terminal users .
- **WeatherBench 2** — Open-source evaluation framework for data-driven weather models with publicly available ground truth data and metrics .

**Frameworks for building custom weather solutions**: Combine **Breezy Weather** for a full-featured Android app with 50+ sources , **Raindrop** for terminal-based forecasting with technical analysis , and **OSS Weather** for privacy-first minimalism . For radar-focused applications, **Supercell Wx** provides native NEXRAD viewing , while **OpenStorm** delivers 3D volumetric visualization , and **Hookecho** offers the most advanced analysis with warning verification . For Windows users, **FluentWeather** provides a native UWP experience . Note that true commercial-grade weather forecasting with proprietary models (AccuWeather MinuteCast, The Weather Channel's Watson AI) and global radar networks remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong visualization, multi-source aggregation, and radar analysis foundations that require integration for complete forecasting systems.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Weather apps access location data and may share it with weather providers. **Review privacy policies before use** — some sources collect more data than others .
- **Severe weather alerts are life-safety critical**. Open-source apps may have delays or miss alerts. Always have a secondary source (NOAA Weather Radio, official government apps) for emergency situations.
- **Radar apps require API keys** for map tiles (MapTiler, Mapbox) — free tiers are available but have limits .
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong visualization, multi-source aggregation, and radar analysis foundations, but **proprietary forecast models and hyperlocal nowcasting** (AccuWeather MinuteCast) remain primarily commercial offerings.
